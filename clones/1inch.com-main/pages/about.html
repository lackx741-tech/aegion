Our company

We're a global community of engineers, designers and problem-solvers, working to realise the potential of Web3.

We research and develop cutting edge technology, then deliver it in seamless products that allow our users and partners to focus on their own strategies and projects, without the distraction of workarounds or security risks.

We work with our partners to advocate for secure, compliant DeFi as the future of finance, for everyone.

Our history

The 1inch story began in 2019, when Sergej Kunz and Anton Bukov built the first DEX aggregator in 56 sleepless hours at an ETHGlobal hackathon in New York.

Since then, we’ve continued to lead DeFi innovation - introducing intent-based swaps in 2022, adding atomic cross-chain with MEV protection in 2024 and the first bridge-free swaps between Solana and EVM networks in 2025.

In late 2025, 1inch launched a new brand identity, centred on our role as leaders in uniting DeFi projects, protocols and liquidity into one seamless ecosystem.
