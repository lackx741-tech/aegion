About 1inch

1inch is the self-custodial DeFi ecosystem building the future of finance. We unite fragmented liquidity across 600+ sources and multiple chains, giving users and institutions secure and efficient control of their assets.

Our team is a global community of engineers, designers, and problem-solvers. We research and develop cutting-edge technology, from smart-order routing to intent-based trading, then deliver it in products that millions trust.

Security

Please note, 1inch will never approach you with an unsolicited invitation to a recruitment process via LinkedIn, Telegram or any other channel. Anyone who does so while claiming to work for us is likely engaging in a social engineering attempt. Do not interact with or download any material they send you.

You can verify any communication by contacting 1inch Support

Information for applicants

We use AI-assisted tools to help us review applications efficiently. These tools support our recruitment team but do not make decisions on our behalf. Every application is reviewed by a person before any outcome is determined. No offer or rejection is made solely on the basis of automated processing. For full details on how we use these tools and your rights in relation to them, please see our Applicant and Candidate Privacy Notice.
